Portfolio risk controls (from your open paper trades)
USD exposure counts EUR, GBP, AUD, NZD, gold and silver pairs against the USD-first pairs (JPY, CAD, CHF). Risk-on counts stocks and bitcoin. This is a rough guide, not a full correlation model.
MT5 order tickets (type these into the MT5 app, demo account)
In MT5: tap the pair, then New Order, and set the type to Buy Stop or Sell Stop. Place both as pending orders and cancel the other when one fills. Your broker may add a suffix to the symbol name. Lot math is approximate and varies by broker.
Market scan (same rule, 10 years, first half / second half)
Paste the scan lines into the box at the top and tap Apply data. Only a market above $1,000 in both halves deserves a second look, and with 13 markets a few will pass by luck.
